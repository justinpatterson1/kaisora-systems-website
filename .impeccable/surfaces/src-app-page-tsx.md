## Direction contract

THESIS: A studio that proves reliability by showing a real production system at work, not by claiming it. Refuses the portfolio "about me" hero and the generic gradient agency splash.
OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground #0B0B0F, charcoal panels #121217 with 1px hairlines at 8% white, off-white grotesk headlines, grey secondary text, mono for technical labels, and one brand purple accent from the owner's Pixel K logo sheet (replacing the comp aqua): #7B2FF7 fills the primary action with white text, and the lifted tint #A87BFF marks live lines, pulses and accent text. A faint grid and a single purple bloom sit behind the hero. No gradients on text, no icon soup.
STORY: The visitor learns that Kaisora builds web, backend and automation systems, sees one running for real, believes it holds up, scans the services and work, meets the founder, and starts a project.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Top nav (Pixel K logo with the KAISORA wordmark, Work, Services, Process, Founder, purple "Start a project"). A left-aligned two-line headline at about 64px, a subtext paragraph, then a purple "Start a project →" and a ghost "See our work". Below them, a full-width "sherryberries / production" panel: five nodes in a row, six branch services, purple pulses travelling along the lines, and a status strip at the bottom.
FORM: Category standard (canon exit), dark developer-tool lineage (Vercel/Linear/Raycast/Supabase/Resend). Seed key fe83f4c2.
SIGNATURE: Requests pulse through the live architecture graph. Reduced motion shows it static.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
